# GoWarmCRM Pricing

> GoWarmCRM is priced flat-rate, not per-seat. One number covers your whole team, with no implementation fees and no per-rep surprises as you grow.

## Plans

### Growth — $999/month

- Billed annually (~$11,988/year)
- Up to 20 reps · 5 manager seats
- Everything in the platform, with no feature gating by seat count

Includes:

- Nightly diagnostic engine across deals, prospects, contracts, cases, and handovers
- Live, prioritised action queue for every rep
- AI-drafted next actions (rep approval required)
- Unlimited playbooks and play chaining
- Standard CRM connectors (Salesforce, HubSpot, Pipedrive, Zoho, Dynamics)
- Email and calendar signal ingestion
- Contract and CLM action queues, prospecting hurdle scores, handover commitment tracking
- Forecast health dashboard, manager action overview, RevOps view
- Per-user AI settings (tone, channel, verbosity)
- Priority support and onboarding

AI usage is metered separately — cost is visible after every call.

### Enterprise — custom

For teams above 20 reps. Everything in Growth, plus:

- Custom integrations and adapter development
- Multi-team configuration
- Bring-your-own AI key (OpenAI, Anthropic, others)
- SSO and advanced security controls
- Dedicated onboarding and SLA-backed support

## Build vs. buy

The build-vs-buy comparison for execution layers typically breaks down as follows for a B2B SaaS team over a three-year horizon:

- **Build in-house**: $800K–$1.5M total cost of ownership (engineering, ongoing maintenance, opportunity cost)
- **License a competitor codebase**: $400K–$600K plus customization
- **GoWarmCRM Growth**: ~$36K over three years, flat, for teams up to 20 reps
- **GoWarmCRM Enterprise**: custom, for teams above 20 reps
